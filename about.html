Liner notes

Born in Kaohsiung and shaped by Taipei and New York, Ryan is an interdisciplinary designer working across motion, graphic and web design, often exploring time. He believes technology opens new and more inventive paths for design.

Ryan Hsieh has a background in Public Relations and Advertising and holds an M.F.A. in Communications Design from Pratt Institute. He has worked as a designer at DTE Studio and KlassiC. Eyewear, and is now a designer at Sat. Knowledge.
